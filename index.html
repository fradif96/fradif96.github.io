About Me

I am a Robotics and AI Researcher specializing in enhancing generalization capabilities of deep learning methods for robotics perception and planning. My research aims at enabling robots to adapt rapidly to unseen tasks using foundation models such as LLMs and VLMs, primarily in collaborative robotics. I hold a Ph.D. in AI for Robotics from the School of Advanced Studies Sant'Anna, where I focused on zero-shot and one-shot imitation learning, RGB-based 3D generative modeling, and 6D pose estimation. Currently, I lead R&D initiatives in Robotics and AI at Almaviva S.p.A., where I drive strategic innovation and technical excellence.
