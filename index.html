Francesco Di Felice

Robotics & AI Researcher

Biographical Sketch

With a Ph.D. in AI for Robotics from the School of Advanced Studies Sant’Anna, I have dedicated my career to advancing deep learning for robotics perception and planning. I held visiting positions at ETH Zurich and led AI R&D teams in industry. Currently, I serve as Robotics and AI R&D Project Manager at Almaviva S.p.A., spearheading cutting-edge initiatives in collaborative robotics.

Research Interests

My research focuses on building robotic systems that can learn from minimal examples—using zero-shot and one-shot imitation learning to mimic human adaptability. I design 3D generative models and novel view synthesis techniques for rich scene reconstruction, and develop category- & instance-level 6D pose estimators for precise manipulation. Additionally, I integrate large language and vision-language models to drive autonomous decision-making in collaborative robotics settings.
