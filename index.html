About Me

I am a Robotics and AI Researcher specializing in enhancing generalization capabilities of deep learning methods for robotics perception and planning. My focus is on enabling robots to adapt rapidly to unseen tasks through foundation models like LLMs and VLMs, particularly in collaborative robotics scenarios.

I hold a Ph.D. in AI for Robotics from the School of Advanced Studies Sant'Anna, where I developed pioneering methods for zero-shot and one-shot imitation learning, and contributed significantly to RGB-based 3D generative modeling and 6D pose estimation. Currently, I lead R&D initiatives in Robotics and AI at Almaviva S.p.A., focusing on strategic innovation and technical excellence.
